## 2025-05-14 - Visual Feedback and Content Integrity in Chat Bots
**Learning:** Visual feedback in chat interfaces, such as Discord's typing indicator, significantly improves perceived performance and user trust during long-running AI generation. Additionally, appending an ellipsis when truncating messages to meet platform limits prevents user confusion about potentially missing information.
**Action:** Always implement typing indicators for asynchronous operations in Discord bots and ensure all content truncation is explicitly signaled to the user.
